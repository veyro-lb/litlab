## Usability evaluation protocol (prepared, not performed)

1. Ask prior-knowledge questions: distinguish internal O–H bonds from between-molecule bonds; explain whether buoyancy and surface tension are the same; predict salt/oil behavior. Record explanations without grades or personal identifiers.
2. Observe a student studying the lesson naturally on a phone and a desktop. Note navigation confusion, diagram legibility, recovery from incorrect answers and ability to find a source.
3. Ask unfamiliar transfer questions: explain capillary behavior in a porous wall; predict warming of equal masses; evaluate a water-vapor claim for an exoplanet. Compare mechanism reasoning with prior responses.
4. Have the student explain structure → interactions → property → consequence in their own words; ask which part remained unclear.
5. Offer a delayed recall task after roughly one week, with participant consent and no invented results.
6. Revise observed problems, repeat the affected checks and have scientific changes re-reviewed. No learning-effectiveness results exist yet.
