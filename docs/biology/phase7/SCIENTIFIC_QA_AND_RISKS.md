# Phase 7 — Scientific and rights QA notes

All 26 sections are original and explicitly mapped, but not certified by IB or an independent reviewer. The official B2.1.9 Guide identifier appears as `B.2.1.9`, while registry identifier is `B2.1.9`.

**Key scientific checks**: lipid heads face water and tails face the nonpolar core; ions are strongly excluded without transport proteins; O2 and CO2 can diffuse directly. Aquaporins are passive water channels, with net osmosis driven by relevant water potential and impermeant solute constraints. Ion channels pass solutes downhill electrochemical gradients, while ATP-linked pumps can drive uphill flux. Plasma membrane glycan chains are extracellular; cholesterol sits within the lipid region and modulates fluidity with temperature. Ligand-gated nicotinic acetylcholine receptors differ from voltage-gated Na+/K+ channels. Na+/K+-ATPase transports 3 Na+ out, 2 K+ in per ATP; SGLT uptake is indirectly dependent on ATP with Na+ gradient coupling, and SGLT isoforms need not share stoichiometry. Cell-adhesion molecules support junctions without fusing entire cells.

**Organelle terminology**: IB guide B2.2.1 includes plasma membrane and ribosomes as organelles and excludes cell wall, cytoskeleton and cytoplasm. Nuclear compartment separation permits mRNA processing before cytosolic translation. Lysosomes isolate acidic hydrolysis and may fuse with phagocytic vacuoles. Mitochondria have matrix Krebs-cycle enzymes, folded inner cristae and intermembrane proton compartment. Chloroplast photosystems sit on thylakoid membranes, with Calvin-cycle enzymes in the stroma. Nuclear envelope disassembly occurs in open nuclear division but not universally in all eukaryotes. Free and RER-bound ribosomes differ mainly by protein destination; Golgi processes/routes and clathrin assembles on the cytosolic vesicle face.
