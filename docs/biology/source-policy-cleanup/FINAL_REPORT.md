Three additional D4.3 scope notes were reviewed. **Teaching hierarchy:** Official IB Biology Guide, first assessment 2025 → Clegg, Davis and Talbot, Hodder Education Third Edition (2023) → focused IB-aligned clarification. Full inventory and scope are enforced by `scripts/biology/source-policy-cleanup/verify.mjs`: 40 topics, 553 understandings, 337 shared SL/HL and 216 additional HL; all 40 lesson packages validated against the central registry without editing it.

## Deliberate non-edits and science integrity decisions

- **C1.3.8** — FACE work is relevant to the prescribed carbon-dioxide-enrichment understanding; it must **not** be removed as collateral during citation cleanup.
- **D4.3.3** — The 120-unit uptake / 150-unit loss carbon-budget exercise is explicitly identified as **illustrative**, not an observed boreal measurement. Kept as a source–sink arithmetic teaching task.
- **D4.3.10 (HL)** — The 126-day/137-day phenology difference is explicitly a **fictional worked calculation**, not observed great-tit or Arctic-plant measurements. Kept under explicit illustrative wording and preserved the two prescribed organism cases.
- **A1.1.8 and B2.3.7–10** — HL content remains present.
- **Water assets:** Existing original-figure attribution to physics references and origin research has not been rewritten as if an IB textbook created those sources. Where referenced only as figure provenance, reference catalog entries may remain visible in the Sources view; they no longer support the removed core narrative.
- **NOAA source policy:** NOAA is not categorically rejected. NOAA monthly measurements are essential for C4.2.20; the D4.3.4 March/September seasonal anomaly table was supplementary rather than prescribed. The real data's observation period, annual/monthly distinction, and attribution are retained.
