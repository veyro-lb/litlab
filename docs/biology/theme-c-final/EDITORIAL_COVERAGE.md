## Scientific issues, decisions and outstanding questions

**C1.1 Enzymes and metabolism.** Catalysts reduce activation barriers without changing reaction energy endpoints; enzyme rate graphs are conditional teaching models, not universal empirical curves; non-competitive allosteric inhibition is not rescued by simply adding substrate.

**C1.2 Cell respiration.** SL C1.2.1–C1.2.6 covers ATP and aerobic/anaerobic comparison without demanding pyruvate or NAD mechanisms. These biochemical details are isolated to C1.2.7–C1.2.17 (HL). O₂ uptake and capillary displacement are proxies and need dimensional conversion; ATP yield is variable.

**C1.3 Photosynthesis.** Chromatographic Rf is dimensionless with shared baseline and stated conditions. Action spectra plot rates rather than pigment absorbance. At HL, water supplies electrons to PSII, the thylakoid lumen accumulates protons and CO₂ limitation indirectly stops sustained photochemistry via carrier recycling.

**C2.1 Chemical signalling (HL only).** Ligand specificity and receptor locations govern signalling; insulin kinase, epinephrine/cAMP and intracellular steroid receptors have distinct pathways. The guide's C2.1.4 label “nitrous oxide” conflicts with biochemical nitric oxide (NO) nomenclature; a qualified expert must resolve this editorial discrepancy.

**C2.2 Neural signalling.** The shared section covers membrane polarization, impulse propagation and basic chemical synapses; detailed voltage-gated channel kinetics, waveform interpretation and inhibitory summation are HL. Correlation r and determination R² require defined data/regression conditions. Neonicotinoid receptor action is not accurately described as simple competitive blocking.

**C3.1 Integration of body systems.** Baroreceptor stretch and chemoreceptor gas/pH sensing are not interchangeable. Medullary regulation and cardiac-output unit analysis were tightened. Plant auxin transport and positive feedback mechanisms are HL; inaccurate tissue-level arrows must go to visual owners.

**C3.2 Defence against disease.** All 18 understandings are shared with no additional HL block. Distinguish innate and adaptive recognition, correct B-/helper-T-cell sequence, HIV fluid transmission versus everyday contact, antibiotic resistance by selection rather than directed mutation, and case percentage change versus percentage difference. COVID examples remain hypothetical where declared.

**C4.1 Populations and communities.** Lincoln estimate requires recapture, mixing and equal catchability assumptions; sigmoid curves are models. Chi-squared association uses independent site presence/absence frequencies, not species individual counts; a significant result is not proof of competition.

**C4.2 Transfers of energy and matter.** Energy flux units must include area and time (for example kcal m⁻² yr⁻¹); a trophic transfer percentage is derived from source fluxes, not an assumed 10% law. The historic Silver Springs values and NOAA Mauna Loa annual means remain identified as empirical sources, unlike calculated ratios or constructed teaching data. Seasonal and multi-year CO₂ changes are distinct.
