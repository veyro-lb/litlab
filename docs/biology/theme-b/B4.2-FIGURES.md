# B4.2 original visual provenance and limitations

Figures `niche-width.svg` and `forest-strata.svg` are newly created BioLab conceptual vector illustrations, not borrowed from the IB Guide or Hodder textbook. `niche-width.svg` deliberately shows a one-dimensional theoretical resource gradient; it is not a measured species-specific niche curve. `forest-strata.svg` shows generalized canopy, understory, epiphytic and liana growth forms, not actual mapped field stands; stranglers are covered in substantive text. Source teaching chapters: IB Biology Guide 2025 printed pp.63–64; Clegg, Davis, Talbot, Hodder Third edition 2023 printed pp.363–379. Independent human review of botanical geometry and ecology remains open. The B4.2.8 prescribed skull/model observation requires authentically sourced, rights-cleared digital hominin collections; written dental comparison is not a substitute for a completed observation activity. Not approved or deployed.
