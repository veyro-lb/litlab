# B4.1 source and visual provenance

IB Biology Guide 2025 B4.1 printed p.62; Hodder Third edition printed pp.341–362. Two new originally authored SVGs were generated: `tolerance.svg` schematizes organism performance across an abiotic range, and `biome-climate.svg` uses conceptual overlapping temperature/rainfall bands with temperature units °C and precipitation units mm/year. They are **not** traced field measurements or a published Whittaker biome dataset. No copyrighted images reproduced. The five-point transect table is explicitly a synthetic teaching dataset and is not evidence that a field transect was performed. Real student-collected field data, calibrated sensors, qualified biome figure checking and rights review remain outstanding. Student-facing teaching and MCQs are original development drafts, not IB-approved content.
