# B3.1 figures and scientific verification

Three original SVG illustrations: `alveolus-capillary.svg`, `dicot-leaf.svg`, `haemoglobin-oxygen.svg`. All consist of newly authored vector paths; no Hodder or IB artwork is reproduced. Diagrams are schematic, not specimens. The haemoglobin chart uses an illustrative Hill saturation model (exponent 2.7; P50 26.8, 19 and 34 mmHg; x-axis kPa via 7.5006 mmHg/kPa); not observed patient data. Authentic calibrated leaf images and practical leaf casts remain necessary to fully assess B3.1.8 and B3.1.10. Figure correctness, accessibility and rights remain independent human-review gates. Sources: IB Guide 2025 pp 57–58, Hodder Third edition chapter B3.1 pp 273–294.
