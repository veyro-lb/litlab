## Authentic case-study source records
- Global moa loss: Holdaway et al. 2014, doi:10.1038/ncomms6436, hunting/habitat inference. Guide specifies North Island giant moa.
- Caribbean monk seal: NOAA's historical 2008 review of an extinct marine species.
- Syrian brown bear: Lebanon CBD first national report documents loss within Lebanon only; *do not assert global extinction*.
- Lowland mixed dipterocarp forest and oil-palm conversion: Edwards et al. 2011 on retained biodiversity in logged forests.
- Lebanese Akkar coastal dunes: published comparison of historical imagery and sand extraction; students have not collected observations.
- Biodiversity monitoring: WWF 2024 Living Planet Index 73% mean *monitored population-index* decline, not the proportion of species extinct.
- EDGE species prioritisation: ZSL EDGE site, ED+GE dimensions.
