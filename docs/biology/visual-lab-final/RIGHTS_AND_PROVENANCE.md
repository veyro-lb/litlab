# BioLab Visual Laboratory — figure provenance and permitted use
Date: 2026-10-18. Target base: `73f6278c6f8d941f2eeb68b836459efe78572a0b`.

## Source hierarchy and prohibited reproduction
The **IB Biology Guide (first assessment 2025)** controls syllabus identifiers and expected content. **Clegg, Davis and Talbot, Biology for the IB Diploma, Third Edition (2023)** is supporting factual instruction, not a licence to reproduce illustrations. The uploaded **IB Biology Drawings.pdf** is a checklist of drawing conventions and named parts, not an authorised source of reusable artwork. No textbook figures, IB pages, handout diagrams, or watermarks have been copied, traced, cropped, or incorporated. Do not market BioLab images as IB-certified.

## Original vector illustrations
The 20 original SVGs under `public/biology/reproduction/` and `public/biology/cell-structure/a22-plant-palisade*` are byte-identical to Git objects from illustration specialist branch `9bd8a38672b1a5bf610d9b224c7363131ab255ed`. They represent four reproductive diagram subjects (male reproductive tract, female frontal, female sagittal, insect-pollinated flower) and one plant-cell subject, each with labelled/recall and mobile variants. The corrected flower replaces an older misleading drawing. Original authorship is documented in `docs/biology/scientific-visual-redesign/FIGURE_PROVENANCE.md` **on the specialist branch**; copy neither its lesson JSON nor its workflow wholesale. Project ownership and commercial reuse of these commissioned/original SVG assets are still subject to the project's own rights review. No qualified biology specialist has provided independent final sign-off.

The newly generated `heart-circuits*.svg` assets are original BioLab conceptual SVG paths created specifically for this branch, with a reproducible generator at `scripts/biology/visual-lab-final/build-circulation.mjs`. Diagram arrows trace fish heart → gill capillaries → systemic capillaries → heart, and mammalian right heart → lung capillaries → left heart → systemic capillaries → right heart. Red and blue are **qualitative oxygenation coding**, not empirical measurements. There is no scale, vessel-diameter measurement, or depiction of individual heart chambers/valve anatomy. The `nucleic-acids/paired*.svg` assets are existing original project artwork surgically amended to show two A–T and three G–C hydrogen-bond indicators. The original review-pending footer remains until a real specialist approval.

## Authentic microscopy: licence and observation constraints
**Human HT29 three fluorescence channels (one microscope field):** source `CellProfiler/examples/ExampleHuman`, sample code `AS_09125_050116030001_D03f00d0/1/2.tif`. The example README expressly states the human cell images are provided by **Jason Moffat** and licensed **CC0**: https://github.com/CellProfiler/examples/blob/master/ExampleHuman/README.md . The licence clarification commit is https://github.com/CellProfiler/examples/commit/ab40cbdb6068b04a7e02a0a3e08042b37433a97c ; the permission discussion is https://github.com/CellProfiler/examples/issues/41 (the specialist handoff records copyright holder David Root). Pixel-identical grayscale PNGs and original TIFFs were copied from specialist branch `6c5ed5adb835f4f1c76c97816d6e26b738c694f7` without changing blob bytes. `d0` = DAPI DNA channel, `d1` = phosphorylated histone H3, `d2` = cell-body channel with specific fluorescent target **not established** in pipeline https://github.com/CellProfiler/examples/blob/master/ExampleHuman/ExampleHuman.cppipe . Image brightness is not natural colour. These three channels are **not** independent samples, and there is **no calibrated micrometre-per-pixel scale**. Do not calculate absolute cell/nuclear dimensions or diagnose mitotic phase from PH3 signal alone. Retain scholarly attribution despite CC0.

**Cucumber leaf six-panel TEM plate:** existing unchanged asset `public/biology/cell-structure/cucumber-leaf-tem.webp`, Git blob `707b75d220dc150d533436686e5425cb768bca8a`. Source Vigani et al. (2015), *PLOS ONE* Figure 3, DOI https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0129141. Article licence **CC BY 4.0** explicitly permits reuse with author/source attribution: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0129141 . Caption: A–C control, D–F iron-deficient cucumber leaf mesophyll ultrathin-section TEM; each panel's **own original 500 nm scale bar** must be used, because magnifications differ. The WebP was already validated in project Phase 9 and is not duplicated or modified. Keep on-image/panel-specific bars, author citation, licence/link, and modality in accompanying factual image attribution.

**Student-facing attribution:** externally licensed microscopy requires factual provenance *with the image*, even if scientific teaching references are limited to IB educational sources. Do not hide authors/licence/source as an ordinary secondary teaching reference. Conceptual SVG drawings and genuine micrographs must be clearly distinguished.

## Unapproved/rejected content
No unlicensed textbook figures, watermarked handout figures, AI-generated fake microscope images, inferred microscopic measurements, or synthetic quantitative metabolic data were introduced. Unacquired candidate micrographs in `docs/biology/authentic-microscopy/RESEARCH_LEDGER.json` on the source branch remain research-only. Licence evidence does not replace independent specimen/morphology verification and does not imply broad approval for other images.
