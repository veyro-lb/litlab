## Scope constraints and factual interpretation

1. The **confirmed failed CI** is RA-001/RA-002. The others are blocked **verification gates or configuration risks**, not proven incidents.
2. Database RLS is enabled on all 31 inspected public tables; student profile/progress policies match `auth.uid()=user_id` for select, insert and update; this is good evidence of row-level isolation, **not** a substitute for auditing privileged functions and SQL GRANTs. Tables with RLS but no policies deny ordinary access by default.
3. A `protect_litlab_profile_contributor_role` trigger expressly prevents direct alteration of `contributor_role` and `reviewer_specialization` by normal `anon`/`authenticated` roles. No direct role escalation was observed.
4. No real student account was accessed; no write/query of private student row values, destructive test, privilege mutation, deployment or merge occurred.
5. Cross-branch specialist reports and prior screenshots constitute historical evidence, never approval of a new SHA. All audit recommendations must be carried out **outside this audit-only branch** with owner's explicit authorization.
